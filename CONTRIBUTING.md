# Contributing to Awesome Autonomous Weapons Systems (LAWS)

We welcome high-quality contributions from international lawyers, defense analysts, computer scientists, and disarmament researchers.

## Guidelines
1. **Academic & Technical Rigor**: Entries must represent verified defense systems, peer-reviewed papers, or primary legal sources.
2. **Neutrality**: Descriptions should focus on architectural capabilities, operational parameters, and applicable international law compliance.
3. **Format**: Follow the standard Awesome-List markdown format:
   ```markdown
   - **[System / Paper Name](URL)** — Concise summary of technical specifications and legal context.
   ```
